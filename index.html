I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Purdue University interested in embedded systems and biomedical instrumentation. My goal centers on one core belief: modern technology shouldn't be a privilege that prioritizes profit over social benefit. I want to build systems that are affordable, durable, and accessible for all. I've worked on projects spanning AI-assisted health tools, circuit design, and instrumentation, and I'm continuing to build toward a career designing technology that reaches the people who need it most, regardless of where they live. Open to conversations, collaborations, and opportunities regarding AI development, urban infrastructure, and assistive technology.
