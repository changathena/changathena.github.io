About

Working with a team of 10 for Purdue University's Engineering Projects in Community Service (EPICS), we developed an AI-assisted medical symptom checker web app with Javascript that analyzes user-input symptoms and zip code to identify possible conditions, cross-reference local outbreak data, and provide local medical resources.

My role in this project was to test AI prompt logic, ensure accurate and relevant responses, and integrate the front-end with back-end API calls.
